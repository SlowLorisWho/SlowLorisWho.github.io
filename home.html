Slow Loris is a Toronto-based jazz fusion group consisting of Joe Morgenstern (keys/synths), Ben Payne (bass) and Will Bentley (drums). Slow Loris (the animal) is a nocturnal, rainforest-dwelling mammal and the only venomous primate in the world. Slow Loris (the band) has figured out a foolproof formula (known in academic circles as the "Slow Loris Method"): Write cool songs and play them so they sound real good. Style-wise, think Weather Report, George Duke, Stevie Wonder, and Herbie Hancock. Now think about a time when you talked over someone else because you were too enthusiastic in the moment. Doesn’t feel good, does it? But it’s ok, we all do it. And the fact that you feel bad about it is how I know you’re a good person who cares about your friends.
